Our story

We built the tool we couldn't afford

ImuAssist started with a familiar complaint: teams need project management, but enterprise suites are priced for enterprises. So we built a calm, affordable toolkit — Kanban, tasks, projects and sprints — from Kathmandu for teams everywhere.

How we got here

From spreadsheet chaos to calm boards

  1. The spreadsheet ceiling

    Every growing team we knew was juggling Excel sheets, WhatsApp messages and sticky notes. Work moved, but visibility didn't.

  2. The price shock

    Standard tools cost more per user than most Nepali teams earn per seat. The r/agile thread said it plainly: 'We built a Kanban app because we couldn't afford Jira.' [CLIENT-COPY: confirm permission before quoting the community thread]

  3. A Nepal-built answer

    We designed ImuAssist around the four things teams actually use — Kanban, tasks, projects, sprints — and stripped the rest.

  4. Free for the ones just starting

    A genuinely free plan for small teams, honest NPR pricing after that, and support that answers when Kathmandu works.

Our mission

Calm project management for the 99% of teams

We believe planning power shouldn't be locked behind enterprise budgets. Every team — an agency of five, a startup of twenty, a classroom of fifty — deserves tools that fit both its work and its wallet.

  • Priced in NPR, not in foreign currency
  • Mobile-first for Nepal's networks
  • Focused features instead of overwhelming suites
