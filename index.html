What's inside

Four core tools. Zero clutter.

Kanban, tasks, projects and sprints — the features teams actually use, tuned for fast setup on Nepal's networks. [CLIENT-COPY: confirm feature set and roadmap before launch]

  • Kanban boards

    Drag cards across lanes that read like terrace steps — To-do, Doing, Done — with colours, due dates and labels.

  • Sprints

    Plan sprints with dates, backlog and burndown views. Close a sprint and roll unfinished work forward in one click.

  • Projects

    Group boards, tasks and files under one project roof. Keep client work, product work and coursework separate.

  • Team & roles

    Invite members, assign owners and set roles so everyone sees the work that matters to them.

  • Due dates & reminders

    Deadlines with smart reminders — the work shouldn't depend on someone checking a chat thread.

  • Quick actions

    Create tasks, move cards and switch views without leaving your flow. Fast even on low bandwidth.
